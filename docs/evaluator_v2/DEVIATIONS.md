### Interpretation note

The pilot reached its preregistered 20 logged calls per condition, but it is a feasibility run with one seed. V1 used six Colab runtime starts. The V2 candidate ledger contains three candidates that passed its recorded evaluator checks and six candidates assigned zero after timeouts (one during preflight and five during timed evaluation); therefore this run does **not** demonstrate empirical rejection of a fabricated-distance candidate. That specific gate is supported by the offline evaluator tests, not by an observed adversarial candidate in this pilot. Do not treat V1/V2 candidate counts or raw best scores as a confirmatory comparison.
